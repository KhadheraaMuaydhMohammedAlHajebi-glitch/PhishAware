# PhishAware 0.6.0: Unit 6 integration, evaluation, and deployment

This release integrates the complete system. All eleven functional requirements are implemented,
the system is packaged for deployment, and it has been measured as it will run in the pilot.
No participant has used it yet: every figure in this release comes from scripted sessions,
which test the software and say nothing about learners.

## Highlights

- **Administrator reporting (FR-11)**: the researcher signs in at `/admin` and sees participation counts, the mean gain with its effect size, error rates by cue, and the mean usability score. Statistics appear only when five participants have finished both assessments, and the CSV export has no identifier and no timestamp.
- **Data protection (NFR-05, NFR-11, NFR-12)**: records are deleted 90 days after consent, backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and deleted after 7 days, and a restore is refused unless the backup is authentic, intact, and a PhishAware database. A separate service runs these jobs every day and reports its own health.
- **Consent form 1.1 and Finish (FR-01, FR-10)**: the form states how long records, backups, and sessions last, and each statement is backed by a test of the mechanism behind it. Finish ends the session on the server, so results cannot be opened by the next person at a shared computer.
- **Deployment (NFR-08)**: one container image, started with Docker Compose behind a reverse proxy that provides HTTPS. Every setting is an environment variable, and the application refuses to start with an unsafe one. `docs/deployment.md` covers installation, operation, upgrade, rollback, and removal.
- **Evaluation (NFR-01, NFR-03, NFR-04)**: a load test and a browser audit run against the deployed stack on every push. `docs/evaluation.md` reports the method and the results.

## Quality

- 233 automated tests (155 new) with 100% statement coverage of `src/` (1,280 statements), on Python 3.11 and 3.13.
- The CI pipeline builds the image, checks that it holds exactly the eleven pinned packages, starts the stack, smoke-tests it over HTTPS, checks the TLS versions, and rehearses the restore procedure.
- OWASP ASVS 5.0, Level 1: 52 of 54 applicable requirements are met, one is an accepted deviation, and one is open and rated low (`docs/security-checklist.md`).

## Defects found during integration and fixed

- A copy of a session cookie stayed valid after Finish or sign-out; the end of a session is now recorded on the server.
- Restoring an empty backup file ended with an unhandled `MemoryError`; it is now rejected with a clear message.
- Backups had no age limit, so a deleted record could survive in them; they now expire after 7 days.
- On a 360 px screen a look-alike address broke at its hyphen, which showed phone users a different item from laptop users; hosts are now rendered as units.
- One click recorded an assessment answer for good; an answer is now chosen and then submitted.
- The focus ring on the top bar and the borders of text fields were below the contrast that WCAG 1.4.11 requires, and the dashboard scrolled sideways at 320 px.
- Pages took more than two seconds on an emulated slow mobile connection; static files are now fingerprinted and cached.

## Upgrade

A database created by 0.5.1 upgrades in place: on start, the application adds the tables that this
release introduces and leaves existing data unchanged. Administrator accounts are created with
`flask create-admin`; none exists by default.

## Known limitations

- The live database file is not encrypted by the application; the deployment guide requires disk encryption on the host. Backups are encrypted by the application.
- A new administrator password is not compared with a list of common passwords (finding S-8, rated low).
- The usability inspection left five findings open, none rated above minor; `docs/evaluation.md` lists them.
