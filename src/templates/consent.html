What you will do

About 25 to 30 minutes: a 12-item pre-assessment, six short lessons, practice scenarios with instant feedback, a post-assessment, and a brief usability survey.

What is stored

A random ID, your answers, your scores, and your usability ratings. Nothing is stored until you agree below.

What is never collected

Your name, email address, student number, passwords, IP address, or demographic details.

How long it is kept

Every record is deleted {{ retention_days }} days after you agree, or at once if you withdraw. Encrypted backup copies exist for up to {{ backup_days }} days longer.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary and never graded. You can withdraw on any page while your session lasts, with no penalty.

Time limit

Your session in this browser ends when you choose Finish, or after {{ session_hours }} hours without activity. After that your answers can no longer be linked to you, so they cannot be withdrawn individually.
